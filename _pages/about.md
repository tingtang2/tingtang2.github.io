Hi! My name is Ting and I'm a second-year PhD student in the Columbia Statistics department. I'm broadly interested in Bayesian statistics and optimizing neural networks, as well as applications in the life sciences. I previously worked on argument mining within the field of natural language processing and was a machine learning engineer working on recommendation systems.

I'm fortunate enough to work with Professor [Micah Goldblum](https://goldblum.github.io/) and Professor [John P. Cunningham](https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/cunningham/) and am part of [CAIL](https://cail.columbia.edu/).
